Our Foundation

The Iseyin Guild & The Loom

Every strip of genuine Aso-Oke that enters our Lagos atelier is loomed by generational master weavers in Iseyin and Oyo. Nothing is machine-printed, mass-milled, or imported imitation yarn.

Double-Heddle Treadle Weaving

Our master weavers operate traditional foot treadles that create tension variations unattainable by modern commercial power looms. The cloth breathes in equatorial warmth and holds sharp geometric folds.

Lurex, Glass & Metallic Weft

We commission bespoke weft spools spun with gold and champagne metallic filament, giving the royal emerald and ivory cloths an incandescent shimmer under Nigerian wedding pavilion spotlights.

French Seams & Silk Lining

Aso-Oke can be abrasive against bare skin. Every Adeola Couture ensemble is fully lined in breathable stretch silk twill, finished with blind-stitched French seams so the bride experiences absolute comfort all day.
