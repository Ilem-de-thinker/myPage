The Atelier & Weaving Guild

Preserving Yoruba weaving traditions for modern celebrations.

Founded in 2018 in Victoria Island, Adeola Couture partners directly with 34 generational loom weavers across Oyo and Abia states. We reject polyester synthetics in favor of 100% natural cotton and mulberry silk warps.

Every bespoke agbada and auto-gele is crafted by master artisans who earn 3x fair-trade wage benchmarks.
