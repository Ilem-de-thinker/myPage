The Royal Silk Agbada is the cornerstone of Adeola Couture's ceremonial collection. Hand-loomed in Iseyin on traditional four-pedal looms, each set requires 32 hours of continuous weaving using double-ply cotton and raw silk yarn.

  • Complete 3-Piece Ensemble: Awosoke outer grand robe, Buba tunic, and tailored Sokoto drawstring trousers.
  • Embroidery: Density-matched satin cord chainstitch across chest plate and neck contour.
  • Weight: 1.85 kg structured drape that holds posture throughout 8-hour wedding receptions.
  • Care: Specialized dry-clean only. Comes with monogrammed breathable garment bag.
